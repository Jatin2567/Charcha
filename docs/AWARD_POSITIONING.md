# Award positioning and evidence

Reviewed 10 November 2026 against [Gnani's awards](https://www.gnani.ai/internship) and [participation terms](https://www.gnani.ai/great-indian-ai-internship-registration-form).

The programme permits **one award maximum per entrant/team**. Categories are not ten product checkboxes: some depend on actual entrant eligibility or genuine public engagement. Charcha's main story is a changed instruction that receives a new translation, fresh listener acknowledgement and retained bilingual record.

| Award | Charcha evidence or dependency |
| --- | --- |
| Main Character Award | Separately dated Tamil, Bengali and Telugu examples support the regional-language story; the principal recorded interaction is Hindi–English. Configuration alone does not establish meaning accuracy. |
| Mixed Vibes Award | Needs demonstrated code-mixed speech. Multilingual routing alone does not prove Hinglish/Tanglish/Benglish quality. |
| Real Deal Award | Everyday coordination: a room changes, participants review the instruction and the recap retains the update. Financial savings and user-impact improvements are not measured. |
| Noise Canceller Award | Needs measured performance on noisy telephonic audio. A simulation or provider marketing claim is not an application result. |
| Two-Person Army Award | Requires an actual eligible teammate and truthful contributions. |
| Solo Ninja Award | Applies to a single-participant entry; disclose AI assistance and dependencies. |
| Jugaad Genius Award | Interpretation integrated with versioned correction, individual acknowledgements and recap is the main creative engineering argument. |
| Small Town, Big Build Award | Actual college/city details must substantiate eligibility. |
| Demo Day Drop Award | Use the separate 60-second social cut. The submission form separately requests a 2–3 minute walkthrough. |
| Crowd Favourite Award | Genuine public engagement on the nominated post; no purchased votes, bots or engagement exchanges. |

Lead with the practical change-of-plan story. Explain shared language branches, ordered playback, reconnect recovery and saved reports as supporting engineering. Keep VoiceShield experimental and digital-arrest references as awareness context, not a prevention result.

Additional category evidence needs separate tests or entrant details. This editorial revision does not spend provider credits or claim universal award eligibility.
