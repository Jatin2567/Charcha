STORY AND EVIDENCE
A workshop instruction changes from room three to room four. The film uses saved Hindi-to-English provider output and the actual Charcha interface in a labelled UI replay. The correction was typed. The test listener acknowledges the original, then separately acknowledges the corrected version; the recap retains both. This is a demonstration of the workflow, not evidence of human comprehension or a legally binding agreement.

The source speech is synthetic test speech, and Asha and Alex are fictional illustrated personas. The presentation includes AI-assisted artwork and offline narration, edited screenshots, animation and audio-reactive graphics. Provider-speech presentation copies play at 85% speed and are level-normalised. Playback in the film is not a real-time latency benchmark.

The small recap strip shows exact excerpts of Tamil, Bengali and Telugu output from separate tests on 31 October 2026. Ellipses mark shortened excerpts. The visible note identifies their separate recording date. They were not produced by the workshop meeting. Ten language choices are configured; this film does not certify quality for every language pair or demonstrate a multi-user load test.
