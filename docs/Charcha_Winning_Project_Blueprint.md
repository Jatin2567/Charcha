# Charcha submission direction

Updated 9 November 2026. This replaces the original aspirational blueprint. Its filename remains for existing links; it is not a prediction of winning an award.

The earlier blueprint proposed sub-800-ms interpretation, instant deepfake interception, fixed jitter/embedding-drift thresholds and fully Indian infrastructure. Those claims were not established and must not be used in a submission. Jitter, shimmer or embedding drift alone cannot establish human identity or synthesized speech.

Charcha helps people hold multilingual browser meetings, review original and translated captions, correct a message for everyone, acknowledge its current version and retain a bilingual record. Gnani recognition and synthesis are integrated with hosted Evon and LiveKit. Personal voices require consented enrollment.

VoiceShield is an experimental CPU classifier. It is not validated fraud prevention or liveness detection. Diagnostic signals and source-record fingerprints do not certify a person, statement or agreement.

The proposed main demonstration is Hindi–Telugu coordination with an available fluent Telugu participant. Hindi–Tamil and Hindi–Punjabi are separate prepared pairs, not completed quality claims. Show one correction, independent read-back and a recap of the final version.

Awards are category-specific and one entrant can receive at most one. Demo Day Drop is a plausible target; Real Deal is strengthened by a useful measured outcome. Code-mixed and noisy-telephony claims need separate evidence. Neither ranking nor an internship offer follows from this plan. See the [official challenge](https://www.gnani.ai/internship).
